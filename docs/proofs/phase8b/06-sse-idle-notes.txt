Proof 6 — the run stream survives past the idle timeout
=======================================================

What was asked: the connection still receives events after an idle period longer
than the previous timeout.

What the recording shows (06-sse-frames.json, captured by the driver through
Chrome DevTools Protocol Network.dataReceived, so these are bytes the browser
actually received, not a server-side log):

  one request   POST http://127.0.0.1:3102/api/engine/agent/openduet/run
  frames        5
  bytes         28310
  offsets (s)   0.00, 5.00, 10.00, 14.00, 14.01
  stream open   14.01 s

Only ONE /run request was opened, so the reply did not arrive on a second
connection after a silent reconnect. The model said nothing for 14 s (the mock's
SLOW8B branch sleeps 14000 ms before answering), which is longer than the 10 s
idle timeout that used to close the stream. The connection received data at 5 s
and 10 s, and the reply's own events at 14 s.

Where the old 10 s limit is written down, in the deployment's own source:

  openbot/server/src/delivery/opentag.ts
    export const AG_UI_HEARTBEAT_MS = 5_000;
    export const BUN_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS = 10_000;
    /** `Bun.serve`'s default idle timeout, which the heartbeat has to beat. */

  and the comment on the heartbeat argument:
    "Under Bun's own idle limit. `Bun.serve` closes a connection that has sent
     nothing for 10 seconds, and at 20 a turn longer than that lost its stream:
     OpenTag read the headers and RUN_STARTED, the socket closed under it, and
     the Slack message went unanswered."

So the previous timeout is 10 s (a 20 s heartbeat could not beat it). The frames
at 5 s and 10 s are the `CUSTOM openbot.working` heartbeat; the reply arrives at
14 s on the same open response and the chat tab renders
"SLOW8B reply arrived after fourteen seconds of model silence" — screenshot
06-sse-idle-survived.png.

Reproduce:

  python driver.py --base http://127.0.0.1:3102 --out <repo>/docs/proofs/phase8b --proof 6
